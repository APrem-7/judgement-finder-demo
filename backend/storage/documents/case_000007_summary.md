## Summary of Facts
The dispute concerned the omission of the second proviso to **Section 43‑B of the Income Tax Act, 1961** by the **Finance Act 2003**. The Commissioner of Income Tax (Person C) and a number of employers (represented by placeholders such as Person A, Person B, etc.) were parties. The central question was whether the deletion took effect only from **1 April 2004** or retrospectively from **1 April 1988**. The lead matter in the batch of civil appeals was **Commissioner of Income Tax vs. M/s. Alom Extrusions Limited** (Civil Appeal No. 7771/2009).

## Legal Issues Raised
- Does the omission of the second proviso to Section 43‑B by the Finance Act 2003 operate prospectively from 1 April 2004 or retrospectively from 1 April 1988?  
- What is the effect of the Finance Act 2003 on the deductibility of employer contributions to provident, superannuation, or other welfare funds under the Income Tax Act?

## Court’s Analysis
The Court examined the pre‑amendment wording of the two provisos to Section 43‑B, which required that contributions be paid **before the filing‑date of the return** and supported by evidence to qualify for deduction. It noted the hardship this created for employers and the Government’s response, reflected in the Report of the concerned organization, leading to the Finance Act 2003. The Court considered the statutory language of the Finance Act, which expressly deleted the second proviso and amended the first proviso **with effect from Assessment Year 2004‑2005** (i.e., from 1 April 2004). No clear legislative intent was found to make the amendment retrospective to 1988, and the principle against retrospective tax legislation was applied.

## Ruling / Holding
The Court held that the deletion of the second proviso to Section 43‑B operated **prospectively from 1 April 2004**, not retrospectively from 1 April 1988. Accordingly, the appeal was allowed, leave was granted, and the delay was condoned.

## Key Legal Principles Established
- Legislative deletions in tax statutes are to be interpreted as **prospective** unless the statute unmistakably indicates a retrospective intent.  
- The **Finance Act 2003** deleted the second proviso to Section 43‑B with effect from **1 April 2004**, thereby relieving employers from the earlier restriction on deduction timing.  
- The amended first proviso now permits deduction of contributions to welfare funds **provided they are paid on or before the due date for filing the return** and the requisite evidence is filed with the return.

## Statutes and Provisions Discussed
- **Income Tax Act, 1961** – Section 43‑B (both original and amended provisos)  
- **Finance Act, 2003** (the amendment/deletion provision)  
- **Section 139** of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (return‑filing requirement)
